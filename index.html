Help Center

Getting Started

How do I find partner restaurants?
Go to the Discover page and browse through our list of partner restaurants. You can filter by cuisine, rating, or open status. Click on any restaurant to view details and available food donations.
How do I collect food from a restaurant?
First, connect with the restaurant by clicking "Connect" on their card. Once connected, you can collect available donations by clicking "Collect Food" on the restaurant profile.

Tracking Impact

How is my impact calculated?
Your food collected (kg) is automatically tracked. CO₂ savings are calculated as 2.5kg CO₂ per kg of food collected. Diversion rate shows the percentage of food collected versus waste generated.
What are waste alerts?
You can set a waste threshold. When your waste exceeds this limit, an audible buzzer alert will sound to help you stay mindful of waste generation.

Voice Features

How do I use voice input?
Click the microphone button next to any input field. Speak clearly, and your speech will be converted to text. Works for food names and waste items.
